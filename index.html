Graduation Requirements ↗
Understand your credit options (21.5 vs. 24 credits)
Planning to attend a 4-year university? Let your Success Coach know so they can place you on the 24-credit path. It is also recommended that you take two years of a world language (we offer Spanish, French, and American Sign Language).

Exams & Assignments

How to take exams, submit assignments, and complete your courses.

To pass a class, your final exam and all essays and projects must be passed with at least 60%. You may exchange a course for another in the same subject within 30 days of assignment.
Completing a course: Once you think you are finished, check your gradebook to make sure all assignments are submitted and graded. Courses update to "Completed" automatically within 48 hours after all work is graded.
